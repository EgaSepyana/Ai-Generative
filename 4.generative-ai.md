# Generative AI
Generative because it can generate new data or content. The word generative highlights the system's ability to create something new rather than process existing data. Every conversation you have with ChatGPT is open ended. The model, trained on extensive text data, generates new and unique real time responses. Similarly, providing Dall-E OpenAI's image generation model with a detailed description will prompt it to create a unique picture tailored to your specifications. This illustrates the core capability of generative AI producing novel content by leveraging patterns learned from training data, a common feature among these models. But AI developers can use several different techniques to achieve generative AI, including large language models, diffusion models, generative adversarial networks, neural radiance fields, and hybrid models are also possible.

## Large Langguage Model
Llms are neural networks trained on vast text volumes learning to predict word relationships, and the next likely word in a sentence, but more on llms in the next section.

## Difusion Models
Diffusion models like Dall-E are advanced AI tools primarily used for generating images and video. During operation, Dall-E begins with a random noise pattern and progressively refines it into a detailed image Applying requested shapes and learned patterns through multiple steps to enhance realism.

## Generative Adversarial Networks
Gans function by comparing two algorithms. One generates content while the other judges its realism. Both algorithms enhance their capabilities, refining the content until it's nearly indistinguishable from reality.

## Hybrid Models 
combines Llms and Gans into one powerful content generation system. Currently, there's an incredible amount of investment in different generative AI initiatives around the world, with the big tech firms leading the pack.
