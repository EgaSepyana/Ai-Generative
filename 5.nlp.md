# NLP
Natural Language Processing, often just abbreviated to NLP, is a field of AI that allows computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language data similar to how we would communicate with one another. There's a wide array of techniques used to process and analyze this data, involving statistics, machine learning, and deep learning.

NLP came to life in around the 1950s, but early methods focused on rule based systems, taking grammatical rules of the language and using this to try and process the text.
